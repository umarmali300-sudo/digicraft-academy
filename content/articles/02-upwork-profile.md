A proposal can get a profile opened. The profile is where many clients decide whether to continue. Clients often skim, and an empty or generic profile gives them a reason to leave. This checklist is about making that page specific. It does not guarantee invites or income.

## 1. Use a real, readable photo

Profiles with a clear photo of the person doing the work are easier to trust than a blank avatar, a logo, or a group picture. Face the camera, crop around the head and shoulders, and use daylight from a window. Dress in a way that fits the clients you want. Skip sunglasses and vacation shots.

## 2. Write a title about the client's outcome

"Graphic designer" or "virtual assistant" is a job label, not an offer. A useful title names the result, the niche, and the skill.

- Weaker: Graphic Designer
- Clearer: Brand identity designer for startups | logos and visual systems
- Weaker: Writer
- Clearer: SEO content writer for SaaS | blog posts aimed at search and signups

## 3. Make the first lines earn the click

Upwork shows only the start of the overview in search. Open with who you help, the result, and how you do it. If you have a real number from past work, include it. Do not invent one.

Then explain your process in a few sentences, list the specialties you actually want to be hired for, and close with a simple next step such as "Message me about the project." Delete lines like "I am a hardworking, passionate freelancer." They do not help a client decide.

## 4. Fill the skill list on purpose

Upwork allows up to 15 skills. Put the skill you most want to be found for first, and use the words clients search. Leave off skills you do not want to be hired for. A long unrelated list makes the profile look unfocused.

## 5. Show a few strong portfolio pieces

Three relevant samples are more useful than a dozen unrelated files. If you have no client history yet, create clearly labeled samples:

- Writers: sample articles in the niche
- Designers: case-style before-and-after explanations
- Developers: a small demo or a public code sample

For each piece, say what the project needed, what you did, and what changed. "Designed a logo" is thin. "Rebranded a coffee shop: logo, menu, and packaging" gives the client a picture.

## 6. Record a short video if you can

Most people skip the intro video, which is why a clear one can help you be remembered. Keep it under about 90 seconds. Say who you help and one true proof point. A phone recording with decent light and clear audio is enough. Talk about the client's problem, not your biography.

## 7. Add history and ask for testimonials

List relevant roles, including internships or volunteer work, so unexplained gaps do not do the talking. After a completed job, ask the client for a short testimonial. Off-platform testimonials are fine if you have permission and they are real.

## 8. Set availability and rate honestly

Turn on an availability badge only when you can reply. A badge you ignore costs trust. For hourly rates, pricing at the very bottom of a niche often attracts difficult work. Beginners can sit a little under the middle of the range they can justify, then raise the rate after several good reviews. There is no rate that guarantees a hire.

## Checklist

- Real, well-lit profile photo
- Title with the client outcome and niche
- Opening lines that say who you help
- Skills ordered by the work you want
- At least three portfolio pieces with context
- Short video intro, if you can record one
- Work history without invented roles
- Testimonials only from real clients
- Availability and rate you can keep

## Mistakes that cost attention

Writing the overview for other freelancers instead of the client, listing every skill you have ever touched, and never updating the page after new work. Refresh the portfolio and overview when your offer changes.

An optimized profile makes each proposal easier to trust. Pair it with a specific proposal, and remember that a careful profile still does not guarantee a reply. See [how to write freelance proposals](/resources/freelance-proposals-that-win-clients-2026).
