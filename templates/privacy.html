Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy describes the information this Digicraft website stores.

Account information

If you create a password account, we store your Gmail address, display name, and a salted password hash. We never store the password itself. Google sign-in, when configured, stores the Google account identifier, your verified Gmail address, and the name Google provides. Profile photos you upload are stored on the server.

Learning and community

We store enrollments, lesson progress, lesson comments, chat messages, and message reports. Chat and comments show your display name, not your email address.

Consultation requests

The free consultation form stores your name, Gmail address, chosen team member, preferred contact method, question, optional time, and consent.

How it is used

We use this information to run accounts, limit member areas to verified users, save progress, and read consultation requests. We do not sell personal information. This site does not include advertising or a third-party analytics tracker. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so the browser requests those files from Google.

Your choices

You can change your display name and photo, sign out, or delete your account from the account page. Deleting an account removes the profile, progress, messages, and consultation requests saved with that email address.
